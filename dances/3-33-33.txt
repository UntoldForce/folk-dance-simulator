3, 33-33
by Steve Zakon-Anderson

Permission: shared everywhere on ContraDB (https://contradb.com/dances/203)
Source: CDS News, January 1993; the original is at https://www.cambridgefolk.org.uk/contra/dances/steve_zakon/three_33_33.html

FormationBase:	Duple Minor - Improper
Progression:	Single
Figures:

A1
(4) 1st neighbors balance & pull by right
(4) 2nd neighbors pull by left
(8) 3rd neighbors right hand balance & box the gnat

A2
(4) 3rd neighbors pull by right
(4) 2nd neighbors pull by left
(8) 1st neighbors swing

B1
(8) circle left 3 places
(8) partners swing

B2
(8) long lines forward & back
(8) robins do si do 1½ ⁋
