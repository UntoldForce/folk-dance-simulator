      References and thanks

      This simulator was built from the generous work of dancers, callers and choreographers who share what they know. Thank you. The descriptions here are in our own words; the sites below are the real authorities.

      The Caller's Box, by Chris Page and Michael Dyck
      The layout dances are written in, and the glossary every figure was checked against (formations too). The sample dance, “111”, is by Chris Page and shared there with full permission.
      ContraLab
      The Contra Timing Chart: how many beats each figure usually takes, used for the timing checks.
      Contrafusion
      How long a pull by takes, and its list of country and contra dance formations.
      English Country Dance Basics, a Tutorial, by George W. Williams V
      The English country dance figures (siding, arming, up a double, heys, the Bentley allemande and more) and the English formations: two- to six-couple sets, triple minor and circles. Shared under a Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 license.
