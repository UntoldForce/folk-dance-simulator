Dancer Bowling

The pins are a contra line, dancing.

  1. Drag anywhere to pull your gold dancer back, then let go to send them up the hall.
  2. Bump dancers who are turning, spinning the same way they are (look for the little arrows), to swing on through. Every one in a row is worth more.
  3. Curve your drag to add spin: ↻ or ↺.
  4. Too hard and it's “oof!”: you lose points.
  5. Come to rest beside someone to join the dance.
